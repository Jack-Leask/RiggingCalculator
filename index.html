Reference aid only. Check every figure against the route plan and profile, the manufacturer's ratings and your company procedure before rigging. Conductor list is from the Prysmian NZ bare overhead conductor catalogue. Where the drawing states a conductor weight, use the drawing.

Method: weight = mass × 9.81 × weight span. Weight span each side = span ÷ 2 + (tension ÷ unit weight) × (height difference ÷ span). Tension from sag = unit weight × span² ÷ (8 × sag). Return wave: tension = mass × (2 × span × returns ÷ time)². Tension at a structure = horizontal tension + unit weight × height above the low point of the span. Still air, no wind or ice. 1 link = 0.2012 m, 1 lb = 4.448 N, 1 lb/ft = 1.488 kg/m.

Before you use this calculator

This is a reference aid. It does not replace a lift plan, an engineer, the route plan and profile, or your company's procedures.

  • You are responsible for checking every figure before rigging.
  • Check loads against the manufacturer's ratings for every item in the load path.
  • Results assume still air, with no wind or ice, and are only as good as the figures entered.
  • Conductor data is catalogue data. Use the figure on the drawing where it gives one.
  • If a result looks wrong, stop and get it checked.
